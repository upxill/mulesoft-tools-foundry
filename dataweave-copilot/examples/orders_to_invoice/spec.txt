Convert an order (with a customer and a list of line items) into an invoice.

Rules:
- Exclude any line item whose "status" is "cancelled" -- only confirmed
  line items appear on the invoice and count toward totals.
- For each remaining line item, compute lineTotal = quantity * unitPrice.
- subtotal is the sum of all included lineTotals.
- tax is 8% of the subtotal.
- total is subtotal + tax.
- Round subtotal, tax, and total to 2 decimal places.

Output shape:
{
  "orderId": <the order's orderId>,
  "customerName": <customer.name>,
  "lineItems": [ { "sku", "description", "quantity", "unitPrice", "lineTotal" }, ... ],
  "subtotal": <number>,
  "tax": <number>,
  "total": <number>
}
